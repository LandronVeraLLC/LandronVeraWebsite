About the Firm

Decades of Trusted Legal Excellence

Landrón Vera LLC is a full-service law firm based in San Juan, Puerto Rico, offering representation across the full spectrum of civil, commercial, and administrative law in both state and federal courts.

Founded by Eileen Landrón and Eduardo Vera, the firm has built a reputation for disciplined advocacy, strategic judgment, and personal commitment to each client. Whether navigating complex litigation, corporate transactions, or sensitive family matters, our attorneys bring the same dedication to every case.

We practice in both English and Spanish, and we are proud to serve the Puerto Rican community and clients throughout the United States.
